Social media refers to interactive online technologies and platforms that enable individuals and organizations to create, share, and exchange information, ideas, and multimedia content across virtual networks. For newcomers to computing, social media serves as a digital bridge connecting people globally, offering real-time communication, networking opportunities, and access to news and community communities.

Social Media Comparison

Facebook

Facebook is a primary global social networking service that connects people with family, friends, and businesses. Companies utilize Facebook to build brand recognition through business pages, organic posts, community groups, and targeted advertisements to reach a wide demographic.

Source: Facebook_icon.png
LinkedIn

LinkedIn is a business- and employment-focused social media platform used for professional networking, career development, and corporate branding. Companies use LinkedIn to highlight corporate milestones, share industry insights, recruit talent, and foster business-to-business relationships.

Source: LinkedIn_logo.png
Instagram

Instagram is a photo- and video-sharing social networking platform emphasizing visual content. Businesses leverage Instagram to showcase product imagery, short-form video stories, and brand lifestyle content to boost visual awareness among mobile-first audiences.
